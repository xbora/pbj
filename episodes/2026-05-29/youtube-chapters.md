## How To Add Chapters On YouTube

1. Open YouTube Studio.
2. Go to `Content`.
3. Click the episode video.
4. Paste the chapter block into the `Description` field.
5. Save the video.

YouTube's official chapter rules:

- First timestamp must start at `00:00`.
- Include at least three timestamps.
- Timestamps must be in ascending order.
- Each chapter must be at least 10 seconds long.
- Manual chapters override automatic chapters.

Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579
