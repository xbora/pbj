## Description

Welcome to Episode 3 of PBJ (Paddy and Bora Jam)!

Anthropic did it again.

In this episode, we talk about Dario Amodei’s new essay on AI, jobs, UBI, data centers, and who should benefit from the upside of frontier AI companies.

Then we get into loop engineering, why “prompting” may not be the right mental model anymore, and how teams of AI agents can plan, build, check, and ship work while humans define the outcome and verify the result.

We also discuss Apple’s new Siri direction, app actions, and whether Siri is becoming an agent orchestrator for the iPhone. If Siri can use apps for you, do we still need apps in the same way?

Finally, we talk about Ramp moving from software into services, AI-native junior employees, agentic trading, private AI stocks, and whether outcome-based pricing could replace the old SaaS subscription model.

Dario’s AI essay, AI replacing tasks not jobs, UBI, data centers, Siri as an agent orchestrator, loop engineering, Claude Code, Codex, forward deployed engineers, Ramp, outcome-based pricing, AI-native employees, and the future of SaaS.
