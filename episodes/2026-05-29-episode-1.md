## Quick Take on Paddy's Format

This is a strong show-prep format because it starts from conversation, not
coverage. The key ingredients to keep are:

- one-line hook for each segment
- why listeners should care
- concrete talking points with numbers or examples
- a real tension/debate angle
- host-specific angles
- questions that let the conversation breathe

The main improvement I would make is adding a small source block to every
segment and separating "confirmed facts" from "take/speculation." That keeps
the vibe loose without making the show vulnerable to PR claims or stale numbers.

## Segment 1 - The Hook

### Triple Whale Moby 2 - "The AI Operating System for Ecommerce"

Emotional hook: AI may be coming for the media buyer, creative strategist, CRO
person, and retention operator at the same time.

Why lead with this: Fresh May 19 ecommerce-specific agent story, very close to
Paddy and Bora's shared customer world. It is also the cleanest "AI replaces
pieces of the marketing team" conversation.

### Setup

Triple Whale launched Moby 2 as an ecommerce operator tied to live store data,
Triple Whale's Context Engine, attribution, persistent memory, automations, and
frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. The claimed action surface
includes Meta ad management, Klaviyo campaigns, inventory forecasting, creative
generation, segment building, and landing pages.

### Talking Points

1. The positioning shift is the story: Triple Whale is moving from analytics and attribution into execution.
2. Context is the moat: live ecommerce data, permanent account memory, category benchmarks, and expert playbooks.
3. "Copilot vs. autopilot" is the adoption wedge. Operators may accept queued recommendations before they accept autonomous spend changes.
4. Agencies should feel this one. If Moby owns media buying, creative testing, landing pages, and retention campaigns, what remains agency-shaped?

### Tension / Debate Angle

If the same platform measuring performance also takes autonomous action, where
does trust come from? Great when it works; scary when tracking is laggy,
attribution is disputed, or the AI optimizes toward the wrong business goal.

### Paddy Angle

Does this replace an agency function, or does it make agency operators more
valuable because someone still has to define strategy, incentives, and brand
judgement?

### Bora Angle

Is this a real vertical agent platform, or a SaaS company adding enough action
buttons to make the old dashboard feel agentic?

### Questions to Bounce

- Would you let it pause ad sets while you sleep?
- Is "AI operating system" the right phrase, or just 2026 SaaS cologne?
- What is the first ecommerce workflow you would trust to autopilot?

### Clip Moment

"Would you put your ad account on autopilot?" Make Paddy answer as an agency
operator and Bora answer as an agent builder.

- table of Moby 1 vs. Moby 2 examples
- simple "dashboard -> copilot -> autopilot" graphic

## Segment 2

### Google/Shopify UCP and Universal Cart - "AI Agents Shop Without Your Website"

Emotional hook: Your Shopify site might become the backup checkout flow, not
the main shopping experience.

Why this fits: This is directly in Gentic territory and creates a clean debate
with Paddy's brand/partner marketing lens.

### Setup

Google used I/O and Google Marketing Live to push agentic commerce: Universal
Cart across Google surfaces, UCP-powered checkout, Google Pay, AI shopping
surfaces, Merchant Center AI performance insights, and expansion into YouTube,
hotels, and food delivery. Shopify's own agentic commerce material frames
Shopify Catalog as the structured data layer that makes merchant products
readable to AI agents.

### Talking Points

1. The ecommerce funnel collapses when discovery, comparison, cart, payment, and post-purchase happen inside an assistant.
2. Structured product data becomes a growth channel. Your PDP is for humans; your catalog metadata is for agents.
3. Google says the retailer remains merchant of record, but that does not answer who owns attention, context, or the customer relationship.
4. For small brands, this is both an opportunity and a flattening force: more distribution, less storytelling.

### Tension / Debate Angle

Agentic commerce may increase conversion while weakening the brand surface.
Consumers get convenience; brands risk becoming rows in a machine-readable
comparison table.

### Paddy Angle

What happens to partner marketing when the "partner" is not a publisher or
creator, but a shopping agent or platform cart?

### Bora Angle

Brands need to become agent-readable. The technical question is MCP/UCP/data
plumbing; the business question is whether that turns brand equity into
structured fields.

### Questions to Bounce

- Would you tell a DTC brand to opt in immediately?
- Does this help challengers get discovered, or does it reward whoever has the cleanest data and cheapest price?
- What becomes more important in an AI shopping world: brand, metadata, reviews, or margin?

### Clip Moment

"What happens to your $50K Shopify redesign when the customer never visits the
site?"

## Segment 3

### YouTube Brandcast and Creator Commerce - "Affiliate Content Becomes Paid Media"

Emotional hook: The most trusted creator recommendation might now be one click
away from becoming a paid ad.

Why this fits: This gives Paddy a strong home-field segment and pairs naturally
with the Hudson Method / creator seeding conversation from the original prep.

### Setup

At Brandcast 2026, YouTube announced creator-led shows, Buy with Google Pay for
connected TV, Affiliate Partnerships Boost, Custom Sponsorships powered by AI,
and multimodal video creation. The interesting bit for PBJ is not the feature
list; it is that organic creator content, affiliate links, shoppable surfaces,
and paid amplification are becoming one loop.

### Talking Points

1. Creator content is becoming ad inventory without losing the creator wrapper.
2. Affiliate Partnerships Boost lets brands amplify organic content where products are already tagged.
3. Connected TV commerce makes "watching" and "buying" feel closer together, especially for impulse-friendly products.
4. AI matching and AI creative tools could make creator campaigns more scalable, but also more generic.

### Tension / Debate Angle

Is this the professionalization of creator commerce, or the slow conversion of
every authentic recommendation into an ad unit?

### Paddy Angle

This is a partner marketing agency story. Does it strengthen relationship-first
partner marketing, or pressure it toward volume, tooling, and paid-media ops?

### Bora Angle

Agents can find creators, draft outreach, score content, and manage reporting.
Where is the durable human edge: taste, negotiation, trust, brand fit?

### Questions to Bounce

- Would you rather have 20 premium creator partners or 500 micro-affiliates?
- Does boosting creator content make it more powerful or less trusted?
- What parts of partner marketing should never be automated?

### Clip Moment

"Is this relationship-first partner marketing, or did YouTube just turn every
creator into performance inventory?"

## Segment 4 - The Closer

### Claude/Google Managed Agents vs. n8n/Zapier - "Describe, Don't Drag"

Emotional hook: The tool that replaces your workflow builder might not look like
a workflow builder at all.

Why close with this: Both hosts have scars here. Paddy has n8n and Docker-agent
experience; Bora is actively building agent/MCP systems.

### Setup

Anthropic described Claude Managed Agents as a hosted service for long-horizon
agent work with stable abstractions around sessions, harnesses, and sandboxes.
Google announced Managed Agents in the Gemini API, where a single API call can
spin up an agent with tools and an isolated Linux environment, powered by the
Antigravity harness and Gemini 3.5 Flash.

### Talking Points

1. Workflow builders are not dead, but the "smart middle" is moving into hosted agent runtimes.
2. The new primitive is less "drag nodes" and more "give an agent state, tools, compute, and permission boundaries."
3. Agents still need plumbing: schedules, webhooks, approvals, alerts, retries, logs, and business-system integrations.
4. The user skill changes from knowing every node to describing intent, edge cases, and guardrails clearly.

### Tension / Debate Angle

Does the agent eat the workflow builder, or does the workflow builder become the
control plane for agents?

### Paddy Angle

As an n8n power user, where does visual orchestration still beat a hosted agent?

### Bora Angle

As an agent/MCP builder, what breaks when people pretend natural language
replaces architecture?

### Questions to Bounce

- What workflow would you never trust an agent to run?
- What workflow is now silly to build node-by-node?
- In 12 months, is n8n a workflow tool, an agent tool, or both?

### Clip Moment

Paddy names one thing he would never trust an agent to do; Bora names one thing
he thinks is now silly to build in n8n.

### Visual Anchors

- n8n canvas screenshot
- Claude Managed Agents architecture excerpt
- Google Managed Agents / Antigravity announcement
- simple "nodes vs. agent runtime" sketch

## Backup Segment

### OpenAI Deployment Company / Codex on Prem - "Even OpenAI Thinks Deployment Is the Hard Part"

### Setup

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Deployment Company on May 11 with more than $4B of
initial investment, Tomoro acquisition plans, and FDEs embedded into customer
workflows. A week later, OpenAI and Dell announced a partnership to bring Codex
closer to hybrid and on-prem enterprise data environments.

### Talking Points

1. This is a spicy counterweight to "AI will make services go away." OpenAI is building a services arm.
2. Enterprises do not just need models; they need workflow redesign, controls, data access, and change management.
3. Codex is expanding beyond code into reports, product feedback, lead qualification, follow-ups, and coordination.
4. The local/on-prem angle says enterprise agents are only useful when they can reach the messy systems where work actually lives.

### Tension / Debate Angle

If OpenAI needs forward-deployed engineers to make AI work in enterprises, what
does that say about "just prompt it" as a business strategy?

### Sources

- https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/
- https://openai.com/index/dell-codex-enterprise-partnership/
